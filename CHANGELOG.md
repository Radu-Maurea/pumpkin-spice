# Changelog

## 0.9.0

- The moon is clickable: it glows, and sometimes it swells up and stares back with a grimacing face, its bloodshot eyes following the pumpkin (which jumps in fright)

## 0.8.0

- Pixel-art night scene behind the Pumpkin Pet: moon, twinkling stars, drifting clouds, hills, a dead tree, a graveyard fence and grass for the pumpkin to stand on
- Tombstones now rise out of the ground
- New setting: pumpkinSpice.pet.background

## 0.7.0

- Evil ghosts show up every few minutes and chase the pumpkin. Click them to swat them away; if they reach it, the pumpkin gets knocked around and turns sad
- New command: Pumpkin Spice: Summon Evil Ghost
- New setting: pumpkinSpice.pet.evilGhostMinutes (0 turns evil ghosts off)

## 0.6.2

- Fix: clicks with a little mouse wobble were treated as drags and did nothing
- Clicks during the trick cooldown now give a small squish instead of nothing

## 0.6.1

- Softer, muted orange for the status bar

## 0.6.0

- Pumpkin gets dizzy (X eyes, wobble, circling stars) after hitting the walls 3 times within 6 seconds
- Bouncier walls and a slidier floor

## 0.5.0

- Ghost trick: 2-3 animated ghosts fly out while a graveyard rises around the pumpkin
- Tricks have a cooldown: a new one can start 1 second after the last one finishes

## 0.4.0

- Pixel-art "Z z z" and "!" marks
- Clicking the pet does a random trick: jack-o'-lantern glow, evil red glare, a swarm of bats, or a rising ghost

## 0.3.0

- Pumpkin Pet falls asleep when you stop typing and jumps awake when you start again

## 0.2.0

- Pumpkin Pet moved to its own tab in the right sidebar, next to the chat
- Pet has gravity: drop it, toss it, watch it bounce

## 0.1.0

- Pumpkin Spice dark color theme
- Pumpkin Spice file icon theme
- Spooky status bar messages and a Halloween countdown
- Pumpkin Pet panel: drag the pixel pumpkin around, click it to make it glow
